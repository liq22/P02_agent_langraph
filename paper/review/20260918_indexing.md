## Additional source-derived consequence

All six base menus are distinct. Thus the tool menu itself can identify the phase at the abstract interface level, although the model may not decode it. Monitor/Revise/Recover share a menu in dynamic definitions.

In event-free, non-replay trajectories without call errors, the first successful read permanently removes Inspect from the reachable phase map. The remaining non-recovery menus expose no second read. Theory07 proves the resulting single-acquisition invariant and states both exceptions: errors reopen Recover; replay can advance to a new sample. This is a restriction witness, not a PHM harm estimate. Another read might be redundant or disallowed by the task's observation scope. No protocol alteration or desired failure outcome is used to claim significance.
