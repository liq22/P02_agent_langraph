## Purpose

This artifact strengthens the P0_02 distinction between scientific questions and engineering questions. It is a local clarity refinement for final-threshold re-review; it does not change hypotheses, add experiments, or claim final submission readiness.

## Boundary Rule

Engineering completion makes the scientific test possible. It does not prove the scientific hypothesis.

Scientific evidence requires a comparison under the same node set, budget, and review rubric. Engineering evidence requires traceable files, provenance, replay paths, and preserved blockers.

## Separation Table

| Item | Engineering Evidence | Scientific Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Node-local records | manuscript, artifacts, verdicts, responses, status files exist and are inspectable | claim-evidence validity improves compared with baselines |
| Scheduler and projections | graph status is a minimal derived projection and not a truth source | phase transitions reduce unsupported claims or hard-fail recurrence |
| Provider/data provenance | model policy, dataset boundary, adapter preflight, and secret hygiene are recorded | formal runs produce selection-eligible evidence under the locked protocol |
| Review and response | each comment maps to a response, revision evidence, or retained blocker | independent reviewer pass rate and hard-fail closure improve without hiding negative evidence |
| Negative evidence | failure registers, keep/discard ledgers, and limitation notes retain rejected or unclear evidence | negative-result retention remains high while unsupported claim count falls |

## Falsification Guard

AutoResearch should fail the scientific claim even if engineering records are complete when:

- manual checklist, prompt-only agent, or ungated multi-agent baselines match claim-evidence validity;
- unsupported claim count is not reduced;
- response coverage is only formal bookkeeping;
- negative or reject evidence disappears from downstream artifacts;
- successful logs, generated prose, graph projections, or synthetic/offline runs are promoted into final research truth.
