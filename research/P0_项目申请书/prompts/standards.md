# P0 problem framing standards

## Purpose

Use these checks when a P0 node is framing background, gap, aims, novelty, constraints, or project value.

## Standards

- Start from an important problem, not from a favorite tool or method name.
- State the gap as a falsifiable claim: what is missing, why it matters, and who would care if it were solved.
- Separate landscape facts, hypotheses, and proposed contributions. Do not let a literature summary masquerade as a contribution.
- Prefer deep reading of a few central papers over broad shallow citation lists.
- Actively look for contradictions, failed assumptions, and negative evidence in prior work.
- Run a novelty and feasibility filter before promoting an idea: nearest prior work, minimum viable test, required data, cost, and likely reviewer objection.
- Keep a short "important problems" list for the node: problems worth revisiting, blocked problems, and problems now ruled out.

## Stop Or Narrow

- Stop if the gap cannot be written in one concrete sentence.
- Narrow if the proposed aim needs unavailable data, unaffordable compute, or unbounded literature search.
- Report uncertainty explicitly when source evidence is thin, contradictory, or not yet verified.
