## Review Questions

### Q1. Does the package clearly state which reviewer roles are simulated?

Yes. Three distinct reviewer roles are defined in `artifacts/review_round_notes.md`
and `artifacts/review_round_index.yaml`:

- `method_reproducibility_reviewer`: checks method variables, protocol, data/code
  availability, artifact provenance, PHMGA/Vibench eligibility, and result-ledger
  traceability.
- `empirical_statistics_reviewer`: checks baselines, uncertainty, repeatability,
  unsupported claims, negative evidence, RM101, selected backend, and Stage C/D gaps.
- `venue_claim_clarity_reviewer`: checks novelty thesis, title/abstract fit, claim
  clarity, citation/figure support, AI/agent disclosure, and limitation language.

Each role has an explicit simulated role description, a focus list, and blocking
checks. The roles are distinct: none collapses into another's checklist dimensions.

### Q2. Are novelty/methods/statistics/format/reproducibility dimensions assigned to explicit lenses?

Yes. `artifacts/review_round_index.yaml` maps checklist dimensions per lens:

- p3-round-001a (method_reproducibility_reviewer): methods, protocol, evidence_eligibility, reproducibility
- p3-round-001b (empirical_statistics_reviewer): results, baselines, uncertainty, negative_evidence, formal_result_eligibility
- p3-round-001c (venue_claim_clarity_reviewer): novelty, title_abstract, citation_figure_support, ai_agent_disclosure, overclaiming

The mapping covers all five required dimensions and does not leave any dimension
unassigned. Formal result eligibility is correctly placed under the empirical
lens rather than the venue lens.

### Q5. Does the round define an issue output format that can feed P3_03/P3_04 action mapping?

Yes. `artifacts/review_round_notes.md` and `artifacts/review_round_index.yaml`
both require these fields for every issue:

- `issue_id`, `lens_id`, `severity`, `claim_or_section`, `evidence_location`,
  `problem`, `required_action`, `target_node_or_artifact`, `validation_gate`,
  `blocks_submission`

This schema is sufficient for downstream P3_03/P3_04 action mapping because every
issue carries a concrete evidence location, a target artifact or node, and a
validation gate. Fatal and major issues must additionally include proposed actions,
which is the minimum contract needed for revision routing.

The `output_target` field in the round index confirms the intended downstream
consumer: `blocker_issue_rows_for_p3_03_p3_04` for rounds 001a and 001b, and
`clarity_and_overclaiming_issue_rows` for round 001c.

   section files or compiled manuscript text as mandatory reviewer inputs.
