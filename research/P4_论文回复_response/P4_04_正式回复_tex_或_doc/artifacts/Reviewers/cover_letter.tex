\subsubsection*{Response drafting rule}
Each response must include a direct answer, the concrete manuscript change location, supporting evidence or revision provenance, and a clear commitment status. Do not describe an experiment, citation, figure, table, or manuscript change as completed unless the corresponding local evidence exists.
