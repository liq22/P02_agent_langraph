# P2 manuscript standards

## Purpose

Use these checks when a P2 node drafts, revises, structures, or exports manuscript content.

## Standards

- Lead with one clear technical idea: what changed, why it matters, and what evidence supports it.
- Build from a claim-evidence matrix. Every major claim needs evidence, limitation, or an explicit placeholder.
- Front-load the skim-reader path: title, abstract, introduction, and hero figure should reveal the main contribution quickly.
- Write methods for replication: data, split, baselines, metrics, implementation details, and statistics belong where readers need them.
- Treat distillation as research work. Remove research debt caused by vague abstractions, overlong explanations, inconsistent terminology, or untraceable claims.
- Related work should synthesize by assumptions, methods, or questions, not list papers one by one.
- Do not overclaim. Prefer a narrower claim backed by evidence over a broader claim that reviewers can puncture.

## Stop Or Narrow

- Stop if the required result, figure, citation, or protocol is missing.
- Narrow if a section needs cross-node evidence that has not been explicitly handed off.
- Mark any citation or factual detail that is not yet verified.
